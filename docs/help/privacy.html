How we use your information

We use your name, date of birth, contact details, NHS number or PPSN and your clinical record to provide your care, send you appointment reminders by text, and run our clinics.

Your records are stored in the UK, or in Ireland if you attend one of our Irish clinics.

We keep your records for 6 years after your last appointment, then delete them.

Your clinician may use a secure AI tool to help draft the summary letter of your visit. Your clinician checks and signs every letter.
