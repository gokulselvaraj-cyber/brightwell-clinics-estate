# Attendance policy (AP-3)

Owner: Clinical Operations. Version 2, effective 1 June 2025.

- A **no-show** is an appointment the patient did not attend and did not cancel.
- If a patient has **3 no-shows in 12 months**, online booking is paused. Reception can still book
  them after a conversation with the patient.
- Late cancellations do not count as no-shows, but may incur the late-cancellation fee.
- Children's appointments are never paused; the clinic contacts the parent or guardian instead.
