# Cancellation policy

Owner: Clinical Operations. Change CHG-4410, effective 1 April 2026.

Patients can cancel or change free of charge up to **48 hours** before the appointment (was 24 hours).
Inside 48 hours the appointment is a late cancellation and the clinic's late-cancellation fee applies
to self-pay patients. Insured patients are not charged; the insurer is told.
